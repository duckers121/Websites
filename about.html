About our school

Rooted in community, built for achievement.

Union High School is a welcoming place where students are encouraged to grow academically, socially, and personally.

Our mission

Union High School exists to support students in becoming confident, resilient, and prepared for their next chapter. We blend strong academics with character development, safety, and a culture of respect.

Our teachers and staff work together to provide meaningful opportunities for student leadership, growth, and lifelong learning.

What students can expect

  • Strong academic expectations and support
  • Personalized guidance for high school success
  • Opportunities for leadership and service
  • Programs that help students prepare for college, work, or military pathways
